# Thermodynamics-Informed Learning Captures Hierarchical Hydrogen-Bond Networks in Deep Eutectic Solvents

## Abstract

> Deep eutectic solvents (DESs) offer a tunable platform for lignocellulose refining, but their
> solvation behavior depends on multicomponent hydrogen-bond networks rather than individual
> constituent properties. We develop TiNN, a thermodynamics-informed neural network that
> hierarchically represents HBA-HBD association and solute accommodation through a learned free-
> energy pathway. Trained on 938 records, TiNN achieves a test-set $R^2$ of 0.917 and generalizes
> to new HBA-HBD combinations and sparse conditions. Ablation identifies solute-specific
> stabilization as the largest contributor to prediction accuracy, while the importance of
> hydrogen-bond compatibility depends on the dissolution mechanism. Experimental measurements of
> glucose, xylan, and Kraft lignin in acidic, basic, and neutral DESs show that TiNN reduces the
> geometric mean solubility error from more than 23-fold with COSMO-RS to approximately 1.5-fold.
> Without training on fractionation data, TiNN also distinguishes DES formulations with high and
> low lignin and hemicellulose removal from corn stover. These results support chemically grounded
> solvent screening for lignocellulose fractionation.
