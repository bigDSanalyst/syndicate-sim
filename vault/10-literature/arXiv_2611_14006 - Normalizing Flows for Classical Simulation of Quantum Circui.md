# Normalizing Flows for Classical Simulation of Quantum Circuits in Phase Space

## Abstract

> We present a novel framework to learn a representation of quantum circuits based on the phase
> space formulation of many-body systems. Given that $N$-qubit states can be represented as
> probability distributions on a smooth $2N$-dimensional manifold, we show how unitary gates can
> be parameterized as normalizing flows between these distributions, opening a new avenue for
> quantum circuit simulation using generative models. We evaluate our method on three common
> families of quantum circuits, and show that its accuracy exceeds that of current state-of-the-
> art neural network methods for large system sizes.
