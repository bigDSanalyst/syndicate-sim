# Geometric-resource bounds and permutation-averaged success probabilities for the WZP algorithm

## Abstract

> Solving linear systems of equations is a fundamental task in machine learning and optimization.
> In this work, we extend the original WZP algorithm from real symmetric linear systems to complex
> Hermitian linear systems. We then study the success probability of the modified WZP algorithm
> from a resource-theoretic perspective. By expressing the postselection success probability as
> the expectation value of a positive operator, we derive upper and lower bounds of the maximal
> success probability over separable, incoherent, and real pure states. These bounds further
> establish connections between the maximal success probability and geometric entanglement,
> coherence, and imaginarity. We further show that, under permutation averaging, the averaged
> success probability is an affine function of the coherence fraction, with its dependence
> determined by the alignment between the success operator and the equal-superposition state. We
> also investigate the preprocessed WZP algorithm under subgroup-constrained preprocessing. For
> the two-level family, the optimal success probability is determined by the maximal projection
> weight onto the orbit of the target projector. Our results provide a unified perspective on the
> WZP success probability under different operational constraints.
