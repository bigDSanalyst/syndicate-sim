# Quantum Evolution Kernel for Node-Attributed Graphs

## Abstract

> We present a novel approach to embed node-attributed graphs onto neutral-atom quantum computers,
> and propose an application to digit recognition. Our approach extends PASQAL's quantum evolution
> kernel (QEK) by introducing a node-level feature encoding based on three-level quantum states.
> To this purpose, we present a prototype showing how the resulting embedding can be leveraged to
> extract correlation-based information for classification tasks. The entire quantum process is
> simulated using Pasqal's Pulser library, which inevitably imposes constraints on the size of the
> experimental datasets. We compare our method with classical baselines to evaluate its potential.
> We show that, for the digit recognition task, our method achieves a higher mean accuracy than
> the more commonly used Jensen-Shannon distance-based methods by utilizing the Wasserstein
> distance.
