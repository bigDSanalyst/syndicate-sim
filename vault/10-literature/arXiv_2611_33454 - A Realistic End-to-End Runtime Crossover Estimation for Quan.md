# A Realistic End-to-End Runtime Crossover Estimation for Quantum-Enhanced Training of Large-Scale Machine Learning Models

## Abstract

> Machine Learning (ML) is one promising application domain for Quantum Computing (QC) speedups,
> but asymptotic complexity considerations often mask the actual engineering costs of actual end-
> to-end workflows. For the use case of training a linearized Neural Network with a Quantum Linear
> Solver, a provably favorable quantum scaling has been suggested. We present realistic resource
> estimates for this problem, accounting for data access, computation, and readout. Our analysis
> examines three increasingly complex benchmarking scenarios, under distinct sparsity-scaling
> assumptions, thereby enabling progressively more realistic resource estimates. We identify
> crossover regimes relative to classical methods and find that crossover points emerge only for
> very large problem sizes, long computation times, and highly restrictive output requirements.
> This work provides a blueprint for evaluating quantum-enhanced ML algorithms under realistic
> hardware constraints and help identifying the bottlenecks that must be overcome to approach
> industrial quantum utility.
