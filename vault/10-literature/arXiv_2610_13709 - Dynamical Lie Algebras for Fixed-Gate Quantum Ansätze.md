# Dynamical Lie Algebras for Fixed-Gate Quantum Ansätze

## Abstract

> Parameterized Quantum Circuits have emerged as a central framework for variational quantum
> algorithms in the NISQ regime, with major applications in quantum simulation, combinatorial
> optimization, and, more recently, quantum machine learning (QML). By developing a new approach
> for computing dynamical Lie algebras associated with quantum circuits combining parametrized
> gates and non-parametrized entangling gates, we aim to extend the applicability of recent
> algebraic results to architectures that are actually used in quantum machine learning.
