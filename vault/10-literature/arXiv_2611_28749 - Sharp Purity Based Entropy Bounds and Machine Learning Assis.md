# Sharp Purity Based Entropy Bounds and Machine Learning Assisted Entropy Estimation

## Abstract

> Purity quantifies quantum-state mixedness, motivating the study of its role in controlling
> entropy variations. A sharp purity-based continuity bound is established for the von Neumann
> entropy of qubit states. The bound is attainable for every admissible purity difference and
> strictly improves the existing purity bound. In higher dimensions, an explicit sharp bound is
> derived within the family of pure states mixed with the maximally mixed state. Further
> continuity estimates are obtained for conditional entropy, quantum mutual information, quantum
> conditional mutual information, and squashed entanglement within specified bipartite and
> tripartite families. An application to machine learning assisted qubit entropy estimation
> illustrates how the sharp bound converts purity estimation errors into rigorous entropy error
> bounds.
