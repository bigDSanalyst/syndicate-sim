# Non-Convex Optimization via Quantum Tunneling Search on a Photonic Chip

## Abstract

> Non-convex optimization plays an important role in modern machine learning since plenty of real-
> world problems have objective functions or constraints with non-convex properties. The non-
> convex nature of landscapes gives rise to numerous local minima and saddle points, dramatically
> hindering the ability of gradient descent-based methods to explore the landscape and obtain
> global optima. Here we present quantum tunneling search (QTS), an algorithm based on quantum
> tunneling walk and local annealing, which effectively explores the non-convex landscape by
> leveraging the quantum tunneling effect. In experiments, we map an objective function to the
> potential term of the waveguide Hamiltonian by controlling the refractive index with the
> femtosecond direct laser writing technique. With a proper input state, the average probability
> distributions on 10-micrometer-spaced 29 x 1 and 21 x 9 waveguide arrays highlight the local
> minima of various 1D and 2D non-convex landscapes, respectively. By further introducing the
> varying Hamiltonian approach, more efficient search and even the identification of the global
> minimum are achieved. Our work experimentally verifies that QTS is a successful and efficient
> non-convex optimization solver, demonstrating quantum speedups over classical stochastic
> gradient descent (SGD), which serves as a practical application of near-term analog quantum
> simulators in the field of machine learning.
