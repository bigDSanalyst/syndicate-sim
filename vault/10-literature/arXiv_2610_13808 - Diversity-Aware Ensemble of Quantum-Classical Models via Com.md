# Diversity-Aware Ensemble of Quantum-Classical Models via Combinatorial Fusion

## Abstract

> Variational quantum circuits (VQCs) are highly sensitive to circuit architecture, yet most
> quantum machine learning pipelines select a single best design and discard the remaining
> candidates. This paper addresses whether the structural diversity across quantum circuit search
> spaces can be used to produce stronger predictions through model fusion. We apply Combinatorial
> Fusion Analysis (CFA), a model fusion framework that selects and combines multiple scoring
> systems using rank-score function (RSF) and cognitive diversity (CD), to a pool of 29
> architecturally diverse classical models and quantum circuits. Using a diversity-aware model
> selection method, we identify subsets of maximally diverse models and combine them with both
> score and rank combination. Experiments on four binary classification benchmarks covering
> different domains demonstrate that CFA-based hybrid classical-quantum fusion consistently
> outperform the best individual model on every dataset considered with improvements up to 6.03%
> in accuracy. When comparing with relevant literatures, the CFA framework further achieves
> superior performance across all four datasets.
