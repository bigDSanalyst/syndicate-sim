# Comment on "Supervised Learning with Quantum Hamiltonian Embeddings" (arXiv:2610.26833)

## Abstract

> We discuss the relationship between quantum Hamiltonian embeddings (arXiv:2610.26833) and prior
> developments in Quantum Cognition Machine Learning (QCML), including QCML's earlier
> implementation on IBM quantum hardware. We consider the novelty claims and the computational
> role of encoding an entire dataset in a single ground state.
