# Modular and Looping Quantum Photonic Processing Unit for Quantum Computing

## Abstract

> Photonic processors are emerging as promising platforms in the race towards the realization of
> practical universal quantum computers. Several architectures with universal capabilities have
> been proposed for their implementation via a photonic platform, each with their own set of
> benefits and drawbacks. In this work we propose an architecture for a universal photonic quantum
> computer. The proposed architecture is modular, allowing for a high degree of flexibility and
> scalability, and relies on a loop-based structure, significantly reducing the number of physical
> components required to implement it experimentally. We prove that our architecture is universal,
> deterministic, and compatible with the quantum circuital model, as well as reprogrammable and
> capable of adaptive measurements. Furthermore, we showcase its functioning via numerical
> simulation on specific examples, by using machine learning techniques on a set of fundamental
> linear and nonlinear optical components to obtain some of the building blocks used in the
> architecture. The obtained results can be used to highlight the benefits and the drawbacks of
> the proposed architecture, as well as the route for future developments towards its physical
> implementation.
