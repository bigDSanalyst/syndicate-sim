# Regret Lower Bound for Online Learning of Quantum States with Logarithmic Loss, with Applications to Quantum State Tomography and Confidence Sequences for Matrix Means

## Abstract

> We study the problem of online learning of quantum states with logarithmic loss, which is a
> quantum generalization of online portfolio selection and has applications to quantum state
> tomography, the task of estimating an unknown quantum state from measurement outcomes. We first
> establish a regret lower bound, showing that the regret rates of Exponential Weights and the
> algorithm of Tseng et al. (2025) have optimal polynomial dependence on the dimension. Our
> analysis leads to two further results. First, our lower-bound proof leads to a quantum state
> tomography algorithm that is sample optimal up to polylogarithmic factors in the sample size.
> Second, we develop an algorithm for constructing confidence sequences for the mean of a matrix-
> valued random variable. A numerical experiment shows that the latter algorithm is competitive
> with existing methods.
