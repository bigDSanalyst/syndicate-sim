# Equilibrium propagation in non-equilibrium quantum systems

## Abstract

> Equilibrium propagation (EP) is a powerful training algorithm for self-learning physical systems
> that is widely used for a variety of machine learning inspired tasks. It relies on the existence
> of an energy function that depends on tunable training parameters, and can be extended to apply
> to many-body quantum systems in equilibrium. A key challenge however is the need to repeatedly
> relax to the system ground state after each parameter update. We show here a route for
> circumventing this problem by extending EP to many-body driven-dissipative quantum systems,
> where the dynamics naturally relaxes to a parameter-dependent non-equilibrium steady state.
> Despite the lack of a free energy, we find that for a class of open systems, a quantum detailed
> balance symmetry still allows for an efficient EP strategy. This relies crucially on a new class
> of non-equilibrium static susceptibility relations that we derive in detail. We analyze examples
> of our new non-equilibrium training paradigm for two experimentally-relevant models: a driven-
> dissipative transverse field Ising model, and a parametrically-driven bosonic lattice.
