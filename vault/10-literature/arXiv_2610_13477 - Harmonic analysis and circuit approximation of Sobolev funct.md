# Harmonic analysis and circuit approximation of Sobolev functions on quantum input spaces

## Abstract

> We derive approximation rates for Sobolev functions defined on the complex unit sphere and
> complex projective space, representing quantum states, and on the special and projective unitary
> groups, representing quantum unitary operations. By using harmonic analysis on compact groups
> and compact homogeneous spaces, we characterize the corresponding Peter--Weyl decompositions and
> Sobolev spaces via irreducible representations and the Laplace--Beltrami operator. Combining
> spectral truncation with finite-depth quantum-circuit approximation, invoking the Solovay--
> Kitaev theorem, we establish quantitative rates that separate the spectral truncation error from
> the circuit approximation error.
