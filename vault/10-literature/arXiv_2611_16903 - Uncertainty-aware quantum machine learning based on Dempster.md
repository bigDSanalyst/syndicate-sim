# Uncertainty-aware quantum machine learning based on Dempster-Shafer theory

## Abstract

> A large body of work in quantum machine learning has centered on characterizing the expressivity
> and performance of quantum models. In contrast, little attention has been paid to the
> reliability of predictions beyond average performance measures such as accuracy. In this work,
> we introduce an uncertainty-aware quantum classification framework based on Dempster-Shafer
> theory. This theory allows to express uncertainty through weights assigned not only to
> individual classification labels but to sets of labels. We introduce a training strategy that
> enables parameterized quantum circuits to learn such assignments directly from data. This
> provides an efficient way to prepare the corresponding quantum states and enables the
> application of different post-processing strategies. In particular, it offers a practical
> setting for quantum Dempster-Shafer fusion procedures with polynomial resource requirements,
> even when the corresponding mass functions are defined over exponentially many subsets. Overall,
> our work represents a step toward enabling quantum models to express uncertainty about their
> predictions, thereby extending the evaluation of quantum models beyond predictive performance
> alone.
