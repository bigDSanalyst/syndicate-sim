# Hybrid Quantum-Classical Optimization for Emergency-Priority Traffic Signal Control

## Abstract

> Emergency-priority traffic signal control requires reducing emergency-vehicle delay while
> limiting disruption to ordinary traffic. This work investigates a finite-horizon QUBO
> formulation and compares Exact QUBO optimization, QAOA, CVaR-QAOA, a quantum machine-learning
> policy, fixed-time control, and max-pressure control in a three-intersection corridor. The study
> evaluates three traffic-demand regimes, examines the effect of the emergency-priority weight,
> and compares quantum solutions with the exact QUBO optimum. The optimization-based controllers
> achieved reliable emergency progression across all tested demand regimes, while max-pressure
> produced lower ordinary-traffic delay. Increasing emergency priority reduced emergency travel
> time but increased congestion, with diminishing improvements at larger weights. CVaR-QAOA
> reproduced the exact QUBO solution more accurately than standard QAOA, although the resulting
> differences in closed-loop traffic performance were comparatively small.
