# Exploring Particle Geodesics in a Warp Drive Spacetime

## Abstract

> Although the Alcubierre Warp Drive is theoretically capable of providing faster-than-light
> travel, it may be difficult to use for this purpose. But is it useful for slower-than-light
> travel? We begin by observing the that the warp bubble will act to protect the ship from dust
> particles and other space debris (a potentially serious hazard even at 10% the speed of light).
> We then explore several modifications of the Alcubierre Warp Drive, e.g. a "deflector shield",
> with the perspective of keeping a ship safe from collisions with particles, projectiles, rogue
> planets, and other dangers of space travel.
