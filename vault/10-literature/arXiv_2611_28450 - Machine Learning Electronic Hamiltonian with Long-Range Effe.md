# Machine Learning Electronic Hamiltonian with Long-Range Effects and External Fields

## Abstract

> Conventional machine-learning electronic Hamiltonian (MLEH) models rely on a local-environment
> approximation and can miss the effects of long-range (LR) interactions and external fields.
> Here, we introduce MLEH-LR, a framework that incorporates these effects into Hamiltonian
> prediction. Benchmarks across materials with different polarizations and dimensionalities
> demonstrate substantial improvements in the accuracy of the predicted Hamiltonians and resulting
> electronic structures. MLEH-LR enables accurate, large-scale, high-throughput electronic-
> structure calculations, as demonstrated for zigzag boron nitride ribbons under transverse
> electric fields. These calculations allow us to formulate a quantitative relation describing the
> joint width and field dependence of the band gap and to reveal the electronic mechanisms
> underlying its field response. Our work provides an efficient method and tool for studying
> electronic structure in materials where long-range electrostatics and external fields play an
> important role.
