# Temporal Soliton Waves in Modern Fiber Laser Systems

## Abstract

> This chapter provides a comprehensive survey of temporal soliton-wave in modern fiber systems.
> Temporal soliton waves constitute a broad class of nonlinear localized structures in modern
> fiber laser systems, arising from different balances among dispersion, nonlinearity, gain, loss,
> spectral filtering, and various forms of cavity coupling. We review the underlying theory, basic
> experimental findings, and practical methods for the generation, real-time characterization, and
> active control of soliton families, with a particular focus on recent progress reported for
> mode-locked fiber-laser platforms. The families covered in the chapter include dissipative
> solitons, soliton molecules (bound states), and dispersion-managed solitons. For each species,
> we focus on the pulse energy, duration, stationary and breathing states, and emerging
> intelligent (machine-learning-assisted) control strategies.
