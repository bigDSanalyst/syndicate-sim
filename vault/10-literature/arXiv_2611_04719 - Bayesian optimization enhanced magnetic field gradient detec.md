# Bayesian optimization enhanced magnetic field gradient detection based on spin-orbit coupled Bose-Einstein condensates

## Abstract

> Magnetic field detection is of great significance in modern fields such as medical diagnostics,
> geological exploration, inertial navigation, and space physics. We have previously investigated
> the expansion dynamics of a spin-orbit-coupled Bose-Einstein condensate. Building on these
> results, here we propose a scheme for magnetic-field-gradient detection by establishing a
> quantitative relationship between the condensate's rotational deflection angle and the magnetic-
> field gradient. In this work, we present the parameter dependence of this scheme and perform a
> systematic optimization. We introduce a Bayesian optimization algorithm to automatically and
> efficiently search for the optimal combination of key parameters, thereby significantly
> enhancing the sensitivity of the magnetic field gradient measurement. The results show that the
> optimized gradiometer improves the sensitivity by a factor of five. This work not only provides
> a new approach for magnetic field gradient measurement but also serves as a reference for
> machine learning-empowered quantum precision measurement.
