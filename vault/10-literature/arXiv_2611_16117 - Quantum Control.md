# Quantum Control

## Abstract

> Quantum Control is the art of steering the fragile dynamics of a quantum system in a precise way
> toward a desired final state or transformation. It is a cornerstone of many quantum technology
> applications and at the same time allows us to shed light on the fundamental connection between
> the energy structure and the time dynamics of quantum systems at the heart of some of the most
> interesting phenomena of quantum mechanics. In this chapter on quantum control, we introduce the
> control problem, go through basic error sources and methods to suppress these errors and then
> discuss state-of-the-art techniques to solve the control problem, like adiabatic methods,
> quantum optimal control and methods of machine learning.
